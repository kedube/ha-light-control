Things to try

  • FloorsSwitch between the whole home, each floor and the garden above the house.
  • RoomsTap a window, or a room on an open floor, to see that room up close.
  • Tapa tile to switch it on or off.
  • Slidesideways across a light to dim it.
  • Holda tile, or tap its color dot, for color and white temperature.
  • Outletshave their own switch. Turning several off takes a second tap.
  • UndoSwitch the lights off, then take it back from the notification.
